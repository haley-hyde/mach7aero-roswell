Meet the Pilot

Twenty years of Navy cockpits, one first season of racing.

Matt "Cube" Menza spent two decades flying EA-6B Prowlers, F/A-18E/F Super Hornets, and EA-18G Growlers for the Navy — 397 carrier landings, 200 of them at night, across four combat deployments. He's logged more than 6,500 flight hours in over 60 aircraft types, first as a Navy test pilot and now as an airline captain and red air aggressor pilot. Roswell is the one line his logbook was still missing: his first season racing.

The Aircraft

An unmodified L-39C, flown exactly as it trains.

Matt races an Aero Vodochody L-39C Albatros — the Czech-built jet trainer that brought turbofan power to a generation of air forces and has anchored Jet Class since the category began. His aircraft, The Patriot, is unmodified and races in extended range tip tank configuration.

Mach 7 Aero

Veteran-owned, Colorado Front Range.

Mach 7 Aero is a flight test and training company founded by Matt and Tara Menza. Backed by Navy and Marine test pilot experience across the F/A-18 and F-35, the team runs flight test support, dynamic flight training, and jet intro programs for pilots and aerospace companies alike.

Roswell Air Races

The National Championship Air Races, first year in Roswell.

After more than sixty years in Reno, the National Championship Air Races move to Roswell, New Mexico for 2026. Jet Class is the fastest division on the course — speeds over 500 mph — and has been built around the L-39 since the category began in 2002.
